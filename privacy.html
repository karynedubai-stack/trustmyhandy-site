Privacy policy

TrustMyHandy is an app that lists tradespeople in the United Arab Emirates recommended by clients who prove they hired them. TrustMyHandy is operated by Karyne de Clercq, Dubai, UAE. Contact: Karyne.declercq@icloud.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored encrypted by our authentication provider), name and phone number.
  • Quote requests: the trade, area, job description, timing, and the name and phone number you choose to share with professionals.
  • Recommendations: the professional's details, the job, its month and amount, your comments, and the documents you upload as proof (invoices, receipts, payment proofs, photos of the work).
  • Professionals: business name, trade, phone, trade license number and issuing authority, areas covered, description.
  • Device: a push-notification token if you allow notifications.
  • Subscriptions (professionals): subscription status and expiry date received from Apple or Google through our payment provider. We never see your card details.

How we use it

  • To verify that a recommended job really happened before publishing a recommendation.
  • To send your quote request to up to 3 verified professionals in your trade and area. Only those professionals see your name and phone number.
  • To check professionals' trade licenses against official registers.
  • To pre-check proof documents with the help of an AI service, Claude by Anthropic, when you agree to it by tapping "Agree & submit". Anthropic processes the documents only to return the check to us and does not use them to train its models. When every check clearly matches, the recommendation can be published automatically; otherwise a person from our team decides. Trade license documents sent by professionals are pre-checked the same way, and a person from our team always decides on licenses.
  • To send account emails (confirmation and password reset codes) and push notifications, and to run the app and its subscriptions.

We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

What is public

A published recommendation shows your first name and initial, the job type, the month, the area, an amount range and your comments, with verification badges. Your phone number, email and proof documents are never public: proof documents are visible only to you and to the TrustMyHandy moderation team.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our infrastructure provider Supabase (servers in Mumbai, India). Proof documents and trade license documents are sent to Anthropic (Claude API) for the automatic pre-check. Account emails are sent through Resend. Push notifications are sent through Expo. Subscriptions are processed by Apple App Store or Google Play and synchronised through RevenueCat.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete my account) or as described on our account deletion page: your account, quote requests, recommendations and proof documents are deleted. Business profiles you own become unclaimed.

Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data in the app, or by writing to Karyne.declercq@icloud.com. We handle personal data in line with the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021).

Children

TrustMyHandy is not intended for anyone under 18.
