Delete your TrustMyHandy account

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open TrustMyHandy and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate.

By email

Write to Karyne.declercq@icloud.com from the email address of your account, with the subject “Delete my TrustMyHandy account”. We delete it within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, phone, password).
  • Your quote requests.
  • Your recommendations and all proof documents (invoices, receipts, photos).
  • Your push-notification token.

What is kept

Nothing linked to you. Business profiles you claimed as a professional stay listed but become unclaimed, without your personal details. Subscription records stay with Apple or Google under their own policies.
